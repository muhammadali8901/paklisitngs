## What PakListings is

PakListings (https://www.paklistings.com) is a free business directory for Pakistan, founded in 2026.
It helps people discover local businesses, companies and services by
city and category, and it helps businesses get found online with their
own public profile page that search engines can index.

Every approved business gets its own single page — an individual
profile with its details, services and customer reviews. New approved
listings go live once approved by an admin.

## Coverage

- **183 cities** across Pakistan, including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad,
  Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta and Gujranwala
- **24 business categories**, including restaurants, real estate,
  healthcare, technology, education, home services and more

## How to find a business

1. Pick a city at https://www.paklistings.com/cities or a category at https://www.paklistings.com/categories.
2. Open a city-by-category page, for example
   https://www.paklistings.com/city/lahore/restaurants/ (city and category pages list the
   businesses in that place and trade).
3. Open a business page, for example https://www.paklistings.com/business/<slug>/, for its
   phone number, address, services and reviews.

## How to list a business

1. Create a free account at https://www.paklistings.com/register and verify your email.
2. Submit your listing at https://www.paklistings.com/add-business (or https://www.paklistings.com/add-company for a
   company, https://www.paklistings.com/add-job for a job, https://www.paklistings.com/add-professional for a
   professional profile).
3. Choose a tier — Basic PKR 99, Premium PKR 199, Featured PKR 299,
   one-time — and pay by Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer with a
   payment screenshot.
4. Your listing goes live after admin approval.

Full pricing: https://www.paklistings.com/pricing.md. Authentication for agents:
https://www.paklistings.com/auth.md.
